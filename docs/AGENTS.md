# LUCH Managed Agents — product concept / P036 UX

LUCH is a free-to-use **tooling layer** for users who already have an AI assistant and want it to act on approved browsers, files, servers and apps.

**Managed Agents** is an **additional service**, not a paywall for browser, terminal or desktop tools. The product is designed for people who want not only access to tools, but **work performed with ownership, continuity, verification, recovery and a clear outcome**.

## The value proposition

A user states a business or technical goal. A managed LUCH Agent can, when separately implemented and authorized, plan bounded work, obtain scoped access, use LUCH execution tools, keep task state, hand off sub-tasks, verify observable outputs, manage retries and deliver a report with evidence.

**Illustrative services** (not an assertion of present public availability):

- **Workflow Agent:** carry a multi-step workflow through approved websites, files and connected apps.
- **Development Agent:** inspect a codebase, prepare an isolated branch, run permitted checks and submit a PR with findings.
- **Operations Agent:** execute approved server maintenance/monitoring routines and report real results.
- **Business Agent:** prepare tasks in approved business systems, with purchase and payment effects separately controlled.
- **Research/QA Agent:** repeat verification, detect discrepancies and document source-backed findings.

These are distinct value propositions from self-directed tool access. Do not imply a fully staffed autonomous AI team exists just because LUCH Peer Bus/Chat Bridge stores messages.

## Customer journey and commercial placement

**Navigation:** Main LUCH site → **Managed Agents**.

Possible journey for a future live service:

1. Explain the outcome and select an agent service, without requiring knowledge of MCP or server administration.
2. Preview what systems, permissions and optional hosted computation are needed.
3. Display bounded scope, costs/limits and the approval policy **before** any paid or consequential work.
4. Explicitly enroll and authorize relevant resources. No silent access to another account or device.
5. Show task timeline, genuine execution receipts, blockers, QA findings, pauses and recovery.
6. Produce a final usable deliverable, not just a claim that a worker has started.

**Pricing and subscriptions: not launched or specified.** This document intentionally has no fabricated price, checkout, fake waiting list or claim of guaranteed current autonomous model execution.

## Core remains free

All self-directed LUCH capability families remain free from feature paywalls under the founder's current policy, subject to sensible abuse/infrastructure limits. Future managed AI labor and compute can be separately priced with clear consent and accounting.

## Acceptance gate

Before a product can be marked for sale: real independent hosted execution, tenant isolation, permission and revocation tests, valid cost controls, QA gate, support, and production owner approval. An ACK, Peer Bus receipt or model-named thread is not authenticated execution.
