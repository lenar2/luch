## Source of the identity
- `website/assets/luch-brand-r22.svg` is a byte-for-byte copy of `docs/brand/r22-11-4/app.svg` on `brand/r22-11-4-provisional-2026-11-16`.
- Original source: Figma R19 letter geometry + R22.8.2 ivory/teal light, exported at R22.11.4. [Original Figma node](https://www.figma.com/design/98sI6aWEEqnNKgasBjR9MR?node-id=216-46).
- **App: design-freeze / provisional. Not approved as canonical website/README logo.** Separate small plugin variant B remains temporary and was not adopted as the public site favicon.
- The former orange `luch-ray.svg` is **not used** in the new standalone preview or draft README headers. It remains on existing production until approval.

## New experience
- The immediate promise: “Your AI can do more than answer” / «Ваш ИИ может больше, чем отвечать».
- Original logo enlarged as the visual anchor, within its own living light field; no invented symbol, old orange star, AI-logo collage or technical status dashboard.
- One first action leads to a documented real outcome, another to authentic connection information.
- Real transcription case: **240 minutes per customer**, with source reference, not a fabricated testimonial.
- Warm ivory, deep mineral teal, restrained typography and visible keyboard focus; reduced-motion support.
- Independent EN/RU layouts, desktop and narrow-screen CSS.
- Existing main website, RU/EN and other subpages unchanged. Design can be propagated after Founder approval only.
