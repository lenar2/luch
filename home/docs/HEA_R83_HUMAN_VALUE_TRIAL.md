# P029 Home R8.3 — proposed prospective human-value trial
Status: proposed after R8.3 author implementation, before human testing. Not historical preregistration, independently signed evidence, or PRODUCT_READY. No human participants observed in this run.

## Job, alternative, and intended outcome
For a nontechnical person responsible for a site, answer: did its homepage respond to the observed request, what does that establish, what is the next useful action, and what should I communicate to the developer? Compare the current workflow with an ordinary browser plus manually composed message and with R8.2 raw metadata. Account creation, consent, recovery, and time cost count against LUCH. A user who does not want an account must be able to say so; do not conceal friction. No AI is connected.

## Proposed trial, pending independent observer and Founder approval
Use consenting participants who genuinely have a site-related task; no invented participant identities or agent simulation. Independent observer sets and freezes the cohort, minimum complete successes, permitted site scope and acceptance thresholds before trial work. Do not use personal/sensitive data, test payment submission, protected systems, or write to any website.

Tasks, with matched cases and order counterbalancing where practical:
- Homepage returned 200: distinguish observed response from an assurance that the whole site works; choose a practical customer journey to check next.
- Site returned an actual 4xx/5xx or redirect on a consenting test site: explain the observation without inventing a cause or bypassing controls; identify a suitable next action.
- Prepare a usable developer handoff containing source, time, status, limits and request for an appropriate next action; show it to the observer, not an external recipient. Demonstrate history and revoke without a new check.

Observe complete end-to-end success, time to meaningful action (include signup), wrong conclusions, help requests, abandonment, confidence calibrated to evidence, and preference vs baseline. Log whether a human would return and why. Have a developer independently judge whether the report is actionable, without seeing variant identity where feasible. Record unsuccessful/partial outcomes, not only successes.

Proposed decision principle: independent owner/observer must approve exact thresholds and minimum complete humans prospectively. If LUCH adds time without improving correct decisions or handoff, narrow or redesign the task; do not expand into AI simply to make it appear useful. If successful, the first potential SO 6.2 slice is a separately consented explanation of actual verified observations, evaluated against these deterministic rules, not an ungrounded chatbot.

Technical checks and mock decision-table inputs are not human evidence. Preserve distinction between observed facts, interpretations, manual suggestions and verified effects. No production promotion from this document.
