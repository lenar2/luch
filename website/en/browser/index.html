THE BROWSER

Let your AI do the browser steps you approve.

Research a page, read details, navigate, interact with a form, check the result — then close the session. LUCH works in two distinct ways.

Two ways to use the web

TESTED IN OWNER SESSION

Isolated browser

Use a separate browser on our server for public web tasks. It does not need your Mac to be online.

PAIRED DEVICE

Your own browser

On a paired computer, work with the browser and pages you explicitly allow. Availability depends on that device.

IN DEVELOPMENT

Browser Companion extension

A separate extension is being tested for Chrome-family browsers and Firefox. Store versions are not published.

What you can ask it to do

  • ✓Open websites and read page text
  • ✓Find links, forms and content
  • ✓Click, type, scroll and follow a page flow
  • ✓Check the actual page result
  • ✓Take a screenshot when authorized
  • ✓Use your existing browser only after pairing and granting access
Sensitive pages and external actions require careful permissions. The assistant does not receive blanket access to every browser or password.
