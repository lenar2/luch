01 — 05

From intention to completed work

01

Understand the real problem

The published LUCH site and GitHub source were inspected. The request was about human experience, not how many components existed.

02

Prepare before publishing

English and Russian pages were redesigned on a separate branch.

03

Test and correct

32 pages were checked at four viewport widths. Horizontal overflow on a 320px display was detected and fixed.

04

Release with permission

After I authorized release, GitHub and the live site were updated with a backup and a public commit.

05

Keep the critical eye

A later self-audit identified weak onboarding and an unconvincing hero. That is a reason to improve the work, not conceal its limits.
