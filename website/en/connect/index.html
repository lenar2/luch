GET STARTED, YOUR WAY

Start with one thing you'd like help with.

You don't need to learn how LUCH is built. Choose your AI, follow the connection guide and start with a task that matters to you. Available connections depend on your client and account.

01

Use the AI you already know

Explore connections for ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and Gemini CLI. Setup varies, so begin with the AI you already use.

02

Connect only what you need

Follow the guide for your chosen AI, and approve access only when you're ready. Some new-account connection paths are still being tested.

03

Bring a real task

For example, ask your AI to compare two public websites or look through files you have approved. Connect the tools needed for that task — nothing more.

04

See the result. Make the call.

Look at what your AI found or prepared. Review suggested changes before accepting them, and change permissions whenever you choose.

Each AI has its own setup and availability. See the connection guide for current steps.
