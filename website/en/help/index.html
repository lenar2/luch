HELP & QUESTIONS

Understand LUCH before you connect anything.

Short, straightforward answers. For now, LUCH is a prelaunch product rather than a general public service.

Can I use LUCH for free?+
The tools are intended to be free for user-directed tasks. Reasonable safety and infrastructure limits may apply. LUCH-hosted agents are a separate future service.
Will LUCH control my whole computer?+
Only if a supported device is paired and relevant permissions are given. Browser and computer access are separate scopes.
Can it operate websites without my laptop?+
Yes, the isolated server-browser workflow has been tested on a public synthetic fixture. Personal logged-in pages may need device-specific authorization.
Is the browser extension in Chrome or Firefox stores?+
Not yet. Engineering test packages are not public store releases.
Can Claude, Gemini or Grok install LUCH from their catalog?+
Not yet. Public directory submissions and independent host login tests are outstanding.
What if something goes wrong?+
Stop the active action, revoke a device authorization and inspect task status. The public support contact and final support policy are still being prepared.
Public support email and response commitments have not yet been finalized. Do not send sensitive logs or credentials through unverified channels.
