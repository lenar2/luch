PRELAUNCH LEGAL DRAFT

Privacy starts with the access you approve.

This page explains intended principles, not a finalized legal Privacy Policy. Operator identity, precise retention schedules and subprocessors must be published before public launch.

What the service may handle

User authorization metadata, scoped tool requests, observed action results and optional device connection state. Some workflows necessarily access browser page text or files you authorize.

Credentials

Do not send passwords to the model. Credential handling depends on connected services, browser sessions and the permissioned device boundary.

Your control

Where supported, you may revoke linked device permissions and release short-lived browser sessions. Formal deletion and retention procedures are not yet published.

What is not finalized

Verified legal operator, exact purposes, retention windows, data residency, subprocessors, contact and rights procedure. This draft is not a substitute for a final published Privacy Policy.

Public directory submission and account sign-up must wait for finalized operator and privacy documentation.
