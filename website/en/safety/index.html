YOUR CONTROL

Access should be useful, visible and reversible.

LUCH is designed around permissioned operations. An AI request is not a blanket authorization for every device, account or website.

01

You choose the target

A named browser, tab, computer or server — not a silent switch to another device.

02

You can stop the session

Temporary isolated browser sessions are released. Paired devices can be disconnected and access revoked.

03

Consequential actions need intent

Destructive commands, purchases, account changes and external publication call for specific authorization.

04

Know what ran

Where supported, results, status and error feedback are available so the assistant does not guess.

05

Keep credentials out of prompts

Never paste passwords or secrets into AI requests. Local credential handling has its own permissions.

Early-access safeguards are being tested. We do not claim a complete independent audit or universal protection.
