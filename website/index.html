03 / THE DIFFERENCE IS TRUST

Permission is the starting point.
Not the fine print.

LUCH does not invent permission or report a message receipt as completed work. It should be clear what was allowed, what was attempted and what could not be done.

I.

You choose the reach

Connect only the account, browser, machine or files needed for the work.

II.

The boundary stays visible

Unpaired or offline devices fail closed. Separate service restrictions are respected.

III.

Results carry their limits

A proposed change is not a production release. A peer-bus ACK is not proof of execution.

What is real today?

LUCH operates in its founder’s connected environment. Independent activation of an arbitrary new client — from sign-in to first authorized action — is not yet confirmed. Supported capabilities depend on host, account and permissions.
