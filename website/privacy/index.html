Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the hosted LUCH Core service and the LUCH client package published at this repository. LUCH is operated by its publisher, Lenar Salimov, as an individual. The service currently offers limited access, not general self-service registration.

What information is processed

  • Sign-in and identity: OAuth account identifiers and permitted identity attributes, which may include email and profile fields depending on the identity provider.
  • Device connections: device identifiers, names, pairing state, capability inventories and online status.
  • Actions requested: tool names and parameters, task/job identifiers, execution status, results, error details, timestamps and related metadata. Depending on the authorized tool, parameters or responses may contain page text, file content, device details, or other personal information.
  • Optional device media: for configured paired devices, limited photo/video metadata and search indexes can be cached. Original media is not part of the public client package.
  • Operations: connection metadata and security/diagnostic logs may be processed by the service and its infrastructure providers.

Why data is used

To authenticate users, enforce account/device permissions, carry out explicitly requested actions, display job results, investigate faults and security incidents, and maintain the service. LUCH does not sell personal information or use the client package to collect account passwords. Do not include secrets in action arguments unless a tool explicitly requires them and you trust its destination.

Where data goes

LUCH uses Supabase for OAuth authentication and hosted infrastructure for operation. Authorized tool data may travel to external websites, connected devices and the AI host (such as Claude) from which you invoke the tool. Those services have their own privacy policies and may operate in different jurisdictions. The GitHub issue tracker is public: its contents are hosted by GitHub.

Retention and deletion

Completed durable execution jobs are eligible for deletion after approximately 7 days under the currently deployed job-cleanup logic; cleanup runs as subsequent durable state changes occur and the system also caps the number of completed jobs. This is not a guaranteed seven-day deletion deadline. Jobs still queued or running may remain until resolved or explicitly removed. Account/device pairing records remain while the account or pairing is maintained. Optional device media metadata caches can remain until refreshed or administratively removed. Security and infrastructure logs may have separate retention periods; a universal maximum has not been established. We do not claim zero retention.

For access, correction or deletion requests, use the private reporting channel and identify the request as a privacy request. Do not include passwords, tokens or sensitive files in an initial message. We may ask for confirmation of account control before acting. Some data must be retained temporarily for security or applicable legal requirements.

Security and user control

The public MCP endpoint uses HTTPS and authorization. Access to a device or account depends on its connection and grants; a plugin installation does not automatically grant device access. Any tool response delivered to Claude is also subject to Claude's own retention and privacy practices. Revoke a grant or disconnect a device to stop future permitted access; this does not automatically delete historical records.

Changes and contact

We publish material changes on this page with an updated effective date. For non-sensitive questions use GitHub Issues; for confidential security or privacy matters use private reporting. These channels require a GitHub account. Please do not submit personal data in public issues.
